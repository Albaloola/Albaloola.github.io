Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a private, self-hosted AI assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used by one person, its owner, to manage their own legal and administrative matters. In that role it is called "Atticus". It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up or account to create.

What the app does

  • Searches and reads the owner's Gmail to find correspondence and evidence relevant to the owner's own legal matters.
  • Prepares email drafts in the owner's Gmail, and sends an email only when the owner asks it to.
  • Reads files from, and saves files to, the owner's Google Drive, such as letters, court papers and evidence.
  • Keeps organised records and chronologies of the owner's matters on the owner's own computer.

Why it needs access to Google data

The owner's legal correspondence and documents are stored in their Gmail and Google Drive. Access lets the assistant find, organise and act on that material at the owner's request. It connects only to the owner's own Google account, through Google sign-in, and the owner can remove access at any time.
