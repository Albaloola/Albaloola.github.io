Hermes Agent privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Hermes Agent is a private, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, Hermes Agent:

  • reads and searches email in the owner's Gmail account;
  • creates email drafts, and sends email only when the owner asks;
  • reads files from, and saves files to, the owner's Google Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner with their own correspondence, records and legal matters. To carry out the owner's requests, content may be processed by AI model providers (Anthropic and OpenAI). It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Hermes Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine learning models.

Where it is kept

Copies are kept on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored there in encrypted form.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
