Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Atticus is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else.

What Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, Atticus reads, searches and drafts email in the owner's Gmail account, sends email when the owner asks, and reads and stores files in the owner's Google Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner with their own correspondence, records and legal matters. To carry out the owner's requests, content may be processed by AI model providers (Anthropic and OpenAI). It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

Copies are kept on the owner's own computer, and access tokens are stored there in encrypted form.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
